## 2024-05-19 - The Art of Wasting Time
**Learning:** By making routine actions unnecessarily long, such as forcing users to hold down a close button for 5 seconds, we can significantly decrease their efficiency and intentionally waste their time, successfully fulfilling the anti-UX goal.
**Action:** Apply "Hold to confirm" logic to basic, low-stakes interactions to maximize the frustrating, molasses-like experience of the interface.
