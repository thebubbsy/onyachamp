ARCHITECTURAL ADVANTAGES

Engineered for Zero Corruption & Maximum Fidelity

Word's rendering engine is notoriously fragile. MarkSmith was built from the ground up to strictly obey the ECMA-376 OpenXML schema.

Zero-Corruption Guarantee

Enforces all required root namespaces (w:, a:, wp:, m:, wpg:, wps:), dynamic relationship management (rId), and $O(1)$ SAX streaming via OpenXmlWriter.

ShapeForge™ Diagram Engine

Translates Mermaid flowcharts, mindmaps, gitgraphs, Gantt charts, quadrant charts, and timelines into native Word vector shapes (<wpg:wgp>). Fully editable inside Microsoft Word.

Native OMML Equations

Translates complex LaTeX math expressions directly into native Microsoft Office Math elements (<m:oMath>) with literal whitespace preservation and high typographical precision.

Reverse-Engineered SmartArt

Parses Office OpenXML .glox layout definitions and compiles JSON nodes into standard Word diagram data parts for executive process flows and hierarchy trees.

Corporate Template Injection

Injects compiled Markdown runs into existing corporate .dotx / .docx templates, automatically inheriting branded fonts, heading styles, headers, and footers.

WCAG 2.1 ContrastGuard

Calculates relative luminance and mathematical contrast ratios in real time, automatically preventing illegible text against dark or light shading (AA 4.5:1 standard).
